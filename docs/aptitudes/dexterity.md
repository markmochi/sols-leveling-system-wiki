# DEX — Dexterity

Dexterity is the path of momentum. Its 50 base ranks grant **+0.3% movement speed each**—up to **+15%**—while its passives improve travel, climbing, item use, ender-pearl repositioning, and sustained movement.

<div class="passive-banner"><div class="passive-banner__icon"><span>DEX</span></div><div><strong>The Wayfarer's Path</strong><p>Best for explorers, archers, skirmishers, and anyone who values positioning over standing still.</p></div></div>

This is an attribute bonus, so it remains useful even when every passive is toggled off.

## Efficient Stride

Reduces exhaustion caused by **land sprinting and jumping**. It does not make food refill more hunger; it makes high-mobility travel consume hunger more slowly.

| Passive rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DEX required | 5 | 12 | 20 | 30 | 40 |
| Player Level | 4 | 10 | 18 | 30 | 42 |
| Exhaustion reduction | 5% | 10% | 15% | 20% | 25% |

## Agile Climber

Improves ladder and vine climbing. Rank V also lets you **hold Sneak without sliding down**, making construction and vertical fights safer.

## Mobile Handling

Reduces vanilla's movement penalty while using items such as bows, shields, food, and potions. **It does not shorten the item's use duration.**

| Passive rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DEX required | 12 | 20 | 28 | 36 | 45 |
| Player Level | 10 | 18 | 27 | 37 | 47 |
| Penalty reduction | 5% | 10% | 15% | 20% | 25% |
| Approx. movement while using | 24% | 28% | 32% | 36% | 40% |

The last row expresses the result relative to ordinary movement, assuming vanilla's usual 20% use-item movement.

## Turning Point

After throwing an ender pearl, this passive may leave a return mark. While it is active, **Sneak + use while holding an ender pearl** returns you to your departure point without consuming the held pearl.

| Passive rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| DEX required | 30 | 35 | 40 | 46 | 50 |
| Player Level | 20 | 27 | 35 | 43 | 50 |
| Mark chance | 15% | 25% | 35% | 45% | 100% |
| Mark duration | 5s | 10s | 12s | 15s | 20s |
| Cooldown | 60s | 52s | 45s | 38s | 30s |

Returning costs **6 hunger points**, deals **5 ender-pearl damage**, and applies Slowness I for 3 seconds. If the exact return spot is blocked, the mod searches upward for a safe landing. No safe landing means no reckless teleport.

## Flow

Keep moving to build up to three Flow stacks. Standing mostly still for more than one second clears the chain; direct damage removes one stack; severe slowing or immobilization clears it.

| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 40 / 30 | 8.0s | +0.25% | +0.75% |
| 2 | 41 / 33 | 7.5s | +0.50% | +1.50% |
| 3 | 42 / 36 | 7.0s | +0.75% | +2.25% |
| 4 | 43 / 39 | 6.5s | +1.00% | +3.00% |
| 5 | 44 / 42 | 6.0s | +1.25% | +3.75% |
| 6 | 45 / 44 | 5.5s | +1.50% | +4.50% |

At three stacks, rank VI reduces sprint exhaustion by 15%, rank VIII adds 10% air control, and rank X preserves sprint through short falls of three blocks or fewer.

!!! tip "Build note"
    DEX 40 is the efficient travel breakpoint. DEX 50 is a dedicated momentum build: guaranteed Turning Point marks and the complete Flow kit require both maximum DEX and Player Level 50.
