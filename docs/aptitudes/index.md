# Choose an Aptitude path

Every path offers a direct stat and five active gameplay passives. The default 150-point budget lets a level-50 character max three paths, but a good build is defined by breakpoints and synergy—not simply by filling three bars.

## Quick comparison

| If you want... | Start with... | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Forgiving early survival | HP 8, then Vitality/DEF gates | The first eight HP ranks grant +6 health; early Fortitude passives begin at low ranks |
| A durable armored frontliner | Vitality + Fortitude | Healing and emergency survival complement direct-hit mitigation and armor durability |
| Fast exploration, faster attacks, or ranged kiting | Dexterity | Direct movement and attack speed, sprint efficiency, climbing, mobile handling, Return Marks, and Flow |
| Potions, trade, caves, and long campaigns | Intellect | Strong utility with Night Eye, Merchant's Wit, Field Study, and Memory |
| Better chest, fishing, and mob loot plus volatile combat | Fortune | Loot Luck can add a full table roll; Fateful Strike, Ego, and Limit Breaker add high-variance combat rewards |
| Reliable raw weapon damage | Train a matching Mastery | Aptitudes are not STR/WEAPON/RANGE damage stats; Masteries handle combat-style scaling |

## How to read the tables

Gate pairs use `Aptitude / Player` order. For example, `20 / 10` means you need rank 20 in that Aptitude and Player Level 10 for that passive rank.

All values use the v1.0.3 balanced defaults. Servers can enable Long Progression Mode to restore the original Player-Level and Aptitude-rank gates, customize Player-Level lists, or change effect values through the active rules.

<div class="codex-callout"><strong>Passive ranks are free.</strong> Meeting a gate lets you claim the rank without spending another currency. Aptitude Points are spent only on the five main Aptitude ranks.</div>
