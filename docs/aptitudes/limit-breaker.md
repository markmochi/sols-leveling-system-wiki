# Limit Breaker

Limit Breaker is Fortune's capstone: when a worthy enemy pushes you near defeat, you may accept a timed duel for extraordinary power. Victory pays out. Failure inflicts a severe Crash.

<div class="codex-callout codex-callout--danger"><strong>This is a deliberate wager.</strong> The passive never triggers from ordinary mobs. Read the offer, target, escape, and victory rules before relying on it.</div>

## How an offer appears

An offer may appear when all of these are true:

- the opponent is a recognized boss, elite, or hostile player;
- that same opponent dealt at least 30% of your maximum health in final damage during the recent 12-second interaction;
- a nonlethal hit leaves you at or below 30% health;
- the passive is enabled and off cooldown.

The offer lasts five seconds. **Hold Sneak for 1.25 seconds** to accept. Ignoring it causes a 60-second offer lockout, not the full Limit Breaker cooldown.

## The challenge

Accepting fully heals you, grants a temporary shield, and applies the listed bonuses. Your damage bonus is halved against enemies other than the marked target. Elite targets also gain +10% movement speed and +25% knockback resistance.

Leaving by dimension travel, teleport item, logout, excessive distance, or timer expiry fails the challenge. If the target disappears for a neutral reason, the mod uses a quarter cooldown instead of treating it as a full defeat.

## What counts as victory

For a non-player target, victory is credited if you land the final blow, deal at least 20% of its maximum health during the challenge, or contribute at least 25% of the damage dealt to it. A hostile player must be defeated by your final blow.

Victory grants:

- healing equal to 15% of maximum health;
- removal of one harmful effect and three seconds of Afterglow;
- bonus Mastery EXP equal to 50% of mastery earned during the challenge;
- bonus Player EXP equal to your normal adjusted share;
- one eligible bonus item drop, with equipment weighted more heavily.

PvP victories have a 30-minute target lockout and never grant bonus loot.

## Failure and Crash

Crash applies for the duration listed in the table:

- 50% less damage;
- 15% less movement speed;
- 40% less healing received;
- 50% more hunger exhaustion;
- all other Luck passives disabled.

!!! tip "Practical advice"
    Accept only when the target is reachable and the arena is stable. A long cooldown begins whether the wager ends in glory or disaster; an escape route based on teleporting will count against you.
