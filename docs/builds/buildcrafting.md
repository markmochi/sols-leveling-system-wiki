# Buildcrafting

There is no single correct build. The useful question is: **what problem should your 150 aptitude points solve?** Choose one defining path, one supporting path, then spend the remainder on a breakpoint or Awakening milestone you will actually use.

## The rules that shape every build

1. **Your budget grows with Player Level.** Level 50 provides 150 total aptitude points.
2. **Each aptitude caps at 50.** A completed build can maximize three paths, or spread points across four or five for hybrid utility.
3. **Passives cost no separate currency.** Meeting both the Player Level and aptitude requirement lets you claim the rank, including all five 10-rank **Awakenings**.
4. **Aptitude bonuses are always useful.** The base HP, armor, movement and attack speed, orb XP, and Luck bonuses apply even if a passive is toggled off.
5. **Masteries are separate.** STR, WEAPON, and RANGE grow through their own combat use, unlock their own ascending Skill Trees, and do not consume aptitude points.

## Sample level-50 builds

These are **templates**, not prescriptions. Each uses all 150 points.

### The Iron Vanguard — 50 HP / 50 DEF / 50 DEX

High health, Second Wind & Rally, Iron Skin + Immunity Surge, and full Flow State Evasion & Afterimage. Pair it with **WEAPON Mastery** (Rooted Stance or Bloodthirst + Duelist/Momentum + Weapon Master) for a relentless frontliner.

**Strengths:** unmatched survival, mobility, attack speed, melee uptime.  
**Trade-off:** no scholar economy or Fortune burst.

### The Wandering Scholar — 40 HP / 20 DEF / 45 DEX / 45 INT

A fast, self-sufficient explorer with the **complete Dexterity and Intellect passive trees**—unlocking Afterimage, continuous Night Eye, +35% potion duration, +15% bonus Mastery EXP, 100% lost-XP retention, and 3-hit **Expose** for +20% target vulnerability.

**Strengths:** utility, rapid Mastery leveling, team Expose debuff, full Flow State.  
**Trade-off:** lighter direct armor scaling.

### The Relic Gambler — 50 HP / 50 DEF / 50 LUCK

A durable Fortune build designed to survive volatile rolls while maximizing **Fortune Favors the Bold** (+20% proc multiplier on Lucky Recovery, Lucky Drop, and Overconfidence) and boss-duel **Limit Breaker** wagers. Especially thematic beside Sol's Relic System.

**Strengths:** boss wagers, bonus loot rolls, burst crits, strong defensive safety net.  
**Trade-off:** lower movement speed unless paired with Kiting or Adrenaline Mastery nodes.

### The Swift Marksman — 45 HP / 15 DEF / 50 DEX / 40 LUCK

Maximum kiting speed, 50% Mobile Handling while drawing bows, Flow State Evasion + Afterimage, full Vitality Last Resort, and early Limit Breaker access. Pair with **RANGE Mastery** (Quick Draw or Steady Aim + Hunter's Mark/Piercing Shot + Marksman Deadeye).

**Strengths:** bow mobility, ranged burst, strong emergency sustain.  
**Trade-off:** lighter Fortitude mitigation.

### The Balanced Adventurer — 30 HP / 30 DEF / 30 DEX / 30 INT / 30 LUCK

Unlocks the early and middle identity of every path plus early ranks of Last Resort, Flow State, and Eidetic Memory. Comfortable for a first playthrough when you want to sample every tree before specializing.

**Strengths:** broad toolkit and low commitment.  
**Trade-off:** misses deep Awakening milestones (Rally, Immunity Surge, Afterimage, Limit Breaker).

## Leveling without regret

- Spend first on immediate needs: HP for dangerous packs, DEX for travel and bow handling, INT for potions and Mastery training, or DEF for heavy melee pressure.
- Plan around **the next reachable passive or Awakening milestone**, not only the eventual rank-50 build.
- Chosen passives can be toggled off without refunding aptitude points or resetting Mastery trees.
- Test drafts in the [Guild Ledger](planner.md) before spending vanilla XP on a respec.

## Respec & reset pricing

- **Aptitude respec:** `100 + (25 × refunded aptitude ranks) + (5 × Player Level)` **raw Minecraft Experience points** (held as a 15-second quote).
- **Mastery tree reset:** `2 + (2 × chosen tiers)` **Vanilla XP Levels** (**4–12 XP Levels** depending on how many tiers are chosen in that Mastery).

Both actions are blocked during the default 10-second combat lock.
