# Buildcrafting

There is no single correct build. The useful question is: **what problem should your 150 aptitude points solve?** Choose one defining path, one supporting path, then spend the remainder on a breakpoint you will actually use.

## The rules that shape every build

1. **Your budget grows with Player Level.** Level 50 provides 150 total aptitude points.
2. **Each aptitude caps at 50.** A completed build can maximize three paths, but spreading points may unlock more early passives.
3. **Passives cost no separate currency.** Meeting both the Player Level and aptitude requirement lets you claim the rank.
4. **Aptitude bonuses are always useful.** The base HP, armor, speed, orb XP, and Luck bonuses apply even if a passive is toggled off.
5. **Masteries are separate.** STR, Weapon, and Range grow through their own activity and do not consume aptitude points.

## Sample level-50 builds

These are **templates**, not prescriptions. Each uses all 150 points.

### The Iron Vanguard — 50 HP / 50 DEF / 50 DEX

High health, maximum defensive tools, and enough mobility to control the fight. Pair it with Weapon Mastery for a reliable frontline character.

**Strengths:** stable survival, pursuit, melee uptime.  
**Trade-off:** no scholar economy or Fortune burst.

### The Wandering Scholar — 40 HP / 20 DEF / 40 DEX / 50 INT

A survival-ready explorer with the full Intellect tree, Flow access, and a healthy buffer. Excellent for cave expeditions, enchanting, brewing, and long progression worlds.

**Strengths:** utility, preserved vanilla XP, mobility, exploration.  
**Trade-off:** limited high-end mitigation.

### The Relic Gambler — 50 HP / 50 DEF / 50 LUCK

A durable Fortune build designed to survive bad rolls and capitalize on Fateful Strike, Overconfidence, and Limit Breaker. Especially thematic beside Sol's Relic System.

**Strengths:** boss wagers, loot, burst, strong safety net.  
**Trade-off:** slow and deliberately volatile.

### The Swift Marksman — 40 HP / 20 DEF / 50 DEX / 40 LUCK

Mobility and ranged positioning backed by moderate sustain and Fortune. Range Mastery supplies the damage specialization without consuming aptitude points.

**Strengths:** kiting, use-item movement, travel, burst.  
**Trade-off:** no complete capstone tree and modest direct defense.

### The Balanced Adventurer — 30 HP / 30 DEF / 30 DEX / 30 INT / 30 LUCK

Unlocks the early and middle identity of every path. This is comfortable for a first playthrough when you want to learn the system before specializing.

**Strengths:** broad toolkit and low commitment.  
**Trade-off:** misses the decisive rank-40 and rank-50 passives.

## Leveling without regret

- Spend first on immediate needs: HP for dangerous packs, DEX for travel, INT for enchanting, or DEF for unavoidable damage.
- Plan around **the next reachable passive**, not only the eventual rank-50 build.
- A passive can be toggled off without refunding aptitude points.
- Respecs cost raw Minecraft Experience points, so test a draft in the [Guild Ledger](planner.md) before committing.

## Respec pricing

The default price is:

`100 + (25 × refunded aptitude ranks) + (5 × Player Level)` raw Minecraft Experience points

The quote is held for 15 seconds. You cannot respec during the ten-second combat lock. When reducing an aptitude below a passive's requirement, that passive's claimed rank is cleared; it can be reclaimed for free when its gates are met again.
